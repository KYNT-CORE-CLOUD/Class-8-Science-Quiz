Nature — HOTS Question 1
A town removes many trees and covers most open soil with concrete. After a period of heavy rainfall, which outcome is most likely?
  • A. Groundwater level rises sharply because concrete absorbs rainwater.
  • B. Surface runoff and chances of flooding increase because less water can soak into the ground.
  • C. Rainfall decreases immediately because trees no longer produce clouds.
  • D. Soil erosion stops because concrete prevents water movement.
Correct Answer: B
Concrete reduces infiltration, while fewer trees also reduce interception and water absorption by roots. More rainwater therefore flows over the surface, increasing runoff and flood risk.
Biology — HOTS Question 2
A muscle cell and a skin cell from the same person contain the same DNA, yet they perform very different functions. What best explains this difference?
  • A. The muscle cell has completely different genes from the skin cell.
  • B. Different genes are active in the two cells, producing different proteins.
  • C. Skin cells do not contain a nucleus.
  • D. Muscle cells use only mitochondrial DNA.
Correct Answer: B
Most body cells contain the same genetic information, but different sets of genes are switched on or off. This causes the cells to make different proteins and develop different structures and functions.
Chemistry — HOTS Question 3
A student has a mixture of iron filings, sand, and salt. Which sequence would separate all three substances most effectively?
  • A. Add water → filter → use a magnet → evaporate the filtrate.
  • B. Use a magnet → add water → filter → evaporate the filtrate.
  • C. Filter first → use a magnet → add water → boil everything.
  • D. Evaporate first → add water → use a magnet → filter.
Correct Answer: B
First remove iron with a magnet. Then dissolve the salt in water, filter out the insoluble sand, and finally evaporate the water to recover the salt.
Physics — HOTS Question 4
Two students push the same box in opposite directions. Student A pushes with 70 N to the right and Student B pushes with 50 N to the left. If friction is 10 N to the left, what is the net force on the box?
  • A. 10 N to the right
  • B. 20 N to the right
  • C. 10 N to the left
  • D. 130 N to the right
Correct Answer: A
The forces to the left total 50 N + 10 N = 60 N. The rightward force is 70 N, so the net force is 70 − 60 = 10 N to the right.
Summary & Key Takeaways
  • Nature: Infiltration helps rainwater enter the soil and replenish groundwater.
  • Biology: Mitochondria release usable energy from food during cellular respiration.
  • Chemistry: Filtration can separate an insoluble solid such as sand from water.
  • Physics: Average speed is calculated by dividing distance by time.
